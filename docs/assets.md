# Parchment asset

`src/assets/parchment.png` is the blank parchment texture embedded in the executable as resource 102. It was generated on 26 October 2026 with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool. There is no image service or network request in the app. The texture is included under the repository's MIT licence.

The renderer caches decoded pixels once per process, preserves the transparent silhouette, and makes the near-opaque paper body solid. Butter and rose paper remain procedural. `docs/paperdesk.png` is generated by Paperdesk itself with `--render-preview`; its text is live app text drawn over the same paper assets.

## Generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: production texture for a native desktop sticky-note app, isolated old parchment sheet. Create one realistic blank antique parchment sheet photographed perfectly straight down, orthographic, almost square, no perspective tilt. It must look like an actual thin old writing parchment: warm pale ivory ochre centre, organic fine fibres and faint uneven clouding, faded tan oxidation toward perimeter, subtle small brown foxing spots mostly at edges, delicate age creases, naturally irregular worn and chipped edges with a few shallow tears. Physically believable, not cartoon, not vector, not symmetrical gradient. Preserve a broad clean pale writing area across the inner 85 percent for dark text. Sheet occupies nearly entire square canvas with only 2 percent transparent padding. All four edges visible, no other objects, no text, no letters, no handwriting, no symbols, no ornamental border, no scroll rollers, no dramatic burned black edges. Very soft contact shadow only, actual transparent background outside sheet. High-quality realistic material texture.
